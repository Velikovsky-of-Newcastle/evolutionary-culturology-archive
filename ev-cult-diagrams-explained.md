The first diagram we will examine beautifully captures the core of Evolutionary Culturology:

fractal HOLON/partons

The visual representation of nested circles underscores

This echoes the foundational principle of Ev Cult that everything, from the smallest quark to the vastest galaxy, adheres to the principles of (1) integration, (2) cooperation, and (3) hierarchical command.
In Mathematics and Geometry, the visual representation of 
nested circles 
is often referred to as "
Apollonian gaskets
" or "Apollonian circle packings." 
This is a fractal construction that consists of an infinite collection of mutually tangent circles. The Apollonian gasket is named after the ancient Greek mathematician 
Apollonius of Perga
, who studied the problem of finding circles that are tangent to three given circles. It also relates to Set Theory, in Ev Cult.

In sum, the diagram encapsulates the Worldview of Evolutionary Culturology, highlighting the interconnectedness, interdependence, and hierarchical structure of all entities (units) in the universe.

Next diagram:
Lastly, the fourth diagram succinctly encapsulates the three foundational Laws governing 
fractal HOLON/partons

These 3 Laws are not just abstract principles - but reflect the inherent dynamics observed across all scales of reality, from atomic interactions to socio-cultural dynamics.

fractal HOLON/parton

Here's a breakdown:
1. Integrate (upwards):
Visual Representation:
 The large upward-pointing arrow indicates movement or influence towards a larger scale or level.
Meaning: 
Entities or units at any given level integrate into the next higher level or scale. This can be seen in how smaller parts come together to form a larger whole or system. For example, cells integrate to form tissues, which then integrate to form organs. Or words form sentences, which form books.
2. Compete/Cooperate (sideways/laterally):
 
Visual Representation: 
The horizontal arrows pointing towards each other and away from each other indicate lateral interactions between entities or units on the same level.
Meaning: 
Entities or units on the same scale level both cooperate and compete with one another. This dual nature can be observed in various domains. For instance, in ecology, species might cooperate within a mutualistic relationship, while simultaneously competing for resources.
3. Control & Command (downwards/inwards):
Visual Representation: 
The downward bifurcating arrows signify influence or governance towards a smaller scale or level.
Meaning: 
Larger entities or units 
command and control 
the smaller units contained within them, which are on the scale level below. This captures the hierarchy of systems where higher-order systems exert control over their constituent parts. For example, an organ system can regulate the function of individual organs within it. Or the core premise of a story (a narrative) controls the plot events (narremes/units of story) within it.

In sum, the diagram visually and succinctly communicates the core principles of how entities relate to, interact with, and influence one another within the 
fractal HOLON/parton
