Suggested Exercises: 
List the new, emergent (additional) abilities of each new 
unit
, on each new (larger) 
scale 
level, of the 
fractal HOLON/parton
. 
Namely, list out: 
What can atoms do, that its individual parts (protons, neutrons, electrons; alone) cannot? 
What can molecules do, that individual atoms (alone) cannot? 
What can macromolecules do, that molecules (alone) cannot? 
What can organelles do, that macromolecules (alone) cannot? 
What can cells do, that organelles (alone) cannot? 
What can tissues do, that cells (alone) cannot? 
What can organs do, that tissues (alone) cannot? 
What can organ systems do, that organs (alone) cannot? 
What can organisms do, that an organ system (alone) cannot? 
What can kin groups (e.g. a family unit, a tribe) do, that a 
single individual organism 
(a person, a plant, another animal) cannot?
What can superorganisms do, that organisms (alone) cannot? 
What can an ecosystem do, that superorganisms (alone) cannot? 
What can a biosphere do, that an ecosystem (alone) cannot? 
What can a nation do, that a state (a smaller part of a nation; alone) cannot? 
And, despite all their differences of scale, are all these units, on all of their individual scale-levels, each governed by the Three Laws of 
fractal HOLON/partons
?

new view of that science

canon

archive

Art Movements

communication media art form
