Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) introduces the concept of 
fractal HOLON/partons

The Three Laws of Fractal HOLON/partons - Applied to the Brain
1.
 
Law #1: Integration Upwards
o
 
The brain integrates into larger systems, including the nervous system, the organism, and, ultimately, the sociocultural environment. For example, sensory inputs are processed to construct coherent perceptions, which are then used for adaptive behaviors within social and cultural contexts.
2.
 
Law #2: Cooperation and Competition Laterally
o
 
Neural regions cooperate to perform complex tasks while competing for dominance in processes like decision-making and attention. Lateral interactions between brain regions reflect this law, as do broader interactions with other individuals in sociocultural systems.
3.
 
Law #3: Command and Control Downwards/Inwards
o
 
The brain exerts control over its subsystems, from networks of neurons to the molecular mechanisms underlying synaptic transmission. It also organizes these processes to produce coherent thoughts, behaviors, and cultural outputs.

New Light: Creativity can be seen as the interplay of cooperation and competition among fractal HOLON/partons within the brain (e.g., neural networks) and between the brain and cultural systems. Unsolved Question: Is free will an illusion, or does the brain exercise true autonomy?

New Light: Ev Cult reframes free will as a balance between command and control downwards (autonomy of the brain as a system) and integration into larger cultural and environmental systems. New Light: Intelligence is framed as an emergent property of hierarchical systems integrating upwards, competing/cooperating laterally, and controlling smaller subsystems.
