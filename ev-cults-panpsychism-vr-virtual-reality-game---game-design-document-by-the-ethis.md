Objective: 
Inspired by Nagel's famous paper 
"
What Is It Like To Be A Bat?
" (1974)
, this game provides an immersive VR experience that allows players to understand what it is like to 
be 
different entities - from 
atoms
, up through 15 different 
scale/size levels
, to 
the multiverse 
- in a scientifically-accurate, engaging, and mind-blowing manner.
 
 
...And

 in the game, you can find out (or, "

), 
what it is like -  
to be: 
a bat-!
 
(And Thank you, Thomas Nagel.)
Core Concepts:
Immersive VR Experience: 
Utilizes VR and haptics (force-feedback) to provide a fully immersive experience.
Educational:
 Accurate scientific representation of the entities/units (
fractal HOLON/partons: i.e., atoms, molecules, cells, organisms, superorganisms, universes, etc.
), and their interactions.
Interactivity:
 Players can interact with their environment, and with other entities on the same scale/size level 
(
fractal HOLON/partons: atoms, molecules, cells, organisms, superorganisms, universes, etc
).
Scalability: 
Experience existence 
from the perspective 
of various entities/units, each representing a different scale-level (size) of unit, in: the 
fractal HOLON/parton
.
Intellectual & Emotional 
Experience: 
The Player frequently experiences the sensation of 
awe
 (accompanied by the physiological experience of: 
goosebumps
), particularly during 
Mission 1 
of each of the 15 Game (Scale) Levels.
 
Mission 1 
in each case is typically focused on 
Law #1 of fractal HOLON/partons
, namely 
Integrate "upwards", 
into a larger unit on the scale-level above
. In other words, the "oceanic feeling" of 
awe
, characterized by: (1) seeing/perceiving, and being 
dwarfed and humbled 
by 
gargantuanly-large things
, and (2) no longer feeling 
"alone"/isolated/an individual/a single solitary unit
, but the overwhelming sensation of 
feeling oneself to now be a part of something much bigger
.
 
This 
Law #1 - 
Integration `upwards' 
phenomenon also literally applies to the scientific meta-meta-discipline of 
Evolutionary Culturology
, because all of individual the Disciplines/Domains (
Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Psychology, Sociology, The Arts/Humanities, Engineering, etc.
) are now no longer isolated, as Ev Cult achieves 
consilience, the unity of all knowledge, 
with its one-size-fits-all (and infinitely scalable) set of:
   
(1) scientific units 
(i.e., fractal HOLON/partons)
;
 
(2) scale/size-levels 
(i.e., fractal HOLON/partons),
 
(3) Three Universal Laws 
(i.e., the 3 scientific Laws of fractal HOLON/partons), 
and
 
(4) 
the same set of 
evolutionary mechanisms/algorithms, 
for all the units 
(i.e., fractal HOLON/partons)
.

Example 
2-Player Co-Op Mode
: Atom Level
Game Scenario Overview:
In this, 
2-Player Co-op (Co-operative Play) Mode
, each of the 2 VR-players controls 
an individual hydrogen atom
...
 
The primary objective is to form a stable molecule by "bonding" together - which will grant them 
special abilities and advantages 
over other atoms and molecules, in the game environment! 
This 
bonding mechanism
 is not just a gameplay feature, but also serves as an educational tool - to explain how 
atoms form molecules, through sharing or transferring electrons.
Gameplay Mechanics:
Electron Sharing: 
Each player's atom has a set number of valence electrons. Players must position their atoms close enough to share electrons, and form a covalent bond.
Energy Levels: 
Bonding increases the energy levels of both atoms, enabling them to perform special moves or abilities that individual atoms cannot, such as 
emitting energy bursts 
to destabilize other atoms or molecules.
Stability:
