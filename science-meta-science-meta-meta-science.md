Introduction
The quest for a unified scientific framework capable of integrating diverse domains of knowledge has long been an aspiration of scholars. E.O. Wilson’s (1998) call for consilience—the unity of knowledge—has been one of the most well-articulated expressions of this goal. 
However, despite significant progress in interdisciplinary studies, no framework has been fully capable of integrating the natural sciences, social sciences, and humanities under one coherent scientific umbrella. 
Enter Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), a meta-meta-scientific paradigm developed by Velikovsky of Newcastle (2016), which achieves this unification by reducing all systems, processes, and cultural units to

Fractal HOLON/partons are complete whole units—whether they are physical, chemical, biological, social, or cultural—that operate across all scale levels.
They follow three universal laws: (1) upward integration, (2) lateral cooperation and competition, and (3) downward/inward control. 
This framework not only incorporates existing meta-scientific disciplines like Systems Science, Cybernetics, and Complexity Science, but also reduces them - and all individual sciences - to a simpler, more unified model. 
Through Ev Cult, we can now achieve true consilience, solving the unity of knowledge problem across all domains.
 
Theoretical Perspective: The meta-meta-science of Ev Cult
 1. The Three Laws of 
fractal HOLON/partons
:
Ev Cult rests on the foundation of fractal HOLON/partons, universal entities that exist in all systems and cultures. These units are governed by three primary laws:
- Law 1 – Upward Integration: Fractal HOLON/partons integrate into larger systems at the scale level above them. For instance, individual systems like cells integrate into larger systems such as organs, and similarly, cultural units like words integrate into larger units like sentences and stories.
- Law 2 – Lateral Cooperation and Competition: Fractal HOLON/partons on the same scale level cooperate with and compete against other units. This dynamic is seen in ecological systems where organisms cooperate within ecosystems while competing for resources, or in cultural systems where books compete for readers' attention but also cooperate in forming larger narratives within genres.
- Law 3 – Downward/Inward Control: Larger systems exert control over their subsystems, which are composed of fractal HOLON/partons. For example, an organism controls the function of its organs, just as a government controls the institutions within a society.
 
2. The Three Scale Levels of Science:
Ev Cult classifies all knowledge into three distinct but integrated scale levels of science:
1. Individual Sciences: These include fields like Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, where specific systems or units of study (such as atoms, cells, or ecosystems) are examined.  
2. Meta-sciences: Fields like Systems Science, Cybernetics, and Complexity Science, that study the overarching principles governing systems in the individual sciences.
3. Meta-meta-science: Ev Cult itself, which unifies all systems and meta-scientific principles by reducing them to fractal HOLON/partons that operate across all domains, both physical and cultural.
