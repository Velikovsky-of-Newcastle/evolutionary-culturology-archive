Each type of agent interacts with the environment and other agents, demonstrating the principles of integration, cooperation/competition, and command/control.
 Key Elements to Watch for:
1. Agent Roles and Movements:
   - Sensory Input Agents (Green Circles): Move randomly to simulate gathering sensory data from the environment.
   - Awareness Agents (Blue Squares): Follow sensory agents to process sensory inputs and recognize patterns.
   - Perception Agents (Yellow Triangles): Follow awareness agents to interpret sensory data and form coherent representations.
   - Self-Reflection Agents (Red Persons): Follow perception agents to evaluate system states and make adjustments.
2. State Changes:
   - Normal State: Agents operate normally, gathering and processing data.
   - Alert State: Agents change color to orange when certain conditions are met, indicating heightened awareness or need for corrective action.
   - Energy Levels: Agents' energy levels decrease in the alert state and increase in the normal state. Agents die when their energy reaches zero.
3. Trails and Interactions:
   - Agent Trails: Each type of agent leaves a trail corresponding to its color, visualizing its path and interactions.
   - State-Driven Interactions: Watch how agents change color and interact based on their state (e.g., alert vs. normal). Self-reflection agents can influence sensory agents' input values when in the alert state.
4. Environment Dynamics:
   - Background Color: The environment patches change color over time, simulating dynamic environmental conditions.
   - Agent Paths: Observe the paths left by the agents against the changing background to understand their movement and interactions better.
 Detailed Observations
1. Sensory Input Agents:
   - Watch how these agents move randomly, collecting sensory data from the environment.

   - Watch their red trails and how they adjust the system when in the alert state.
