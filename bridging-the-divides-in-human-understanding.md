Top Ten Characteristics of Science
#
 
Characteristic
of Science
Detail
1
Empirical Evidence
Science is based on observable phenomena and must be capable of being
  tested for its validity by other researchers working under the same
  conditions.
2
Reproducibility
The results of experiments and observations must be reproducible.
  Other scientists should be able to perform the same experiment under the same
  conditions and achieve the same results.
3
Falsifiability
Scientific theories must be falsifiable. This means that there must be
  a possible negative answer to the hypothesis. A theory that cannot be proven
  false, that is immune to potential contradiction, is not scientific.
4
Predictability
Science must be able to make predictions. Scientists use their
  theories to predict what they expect to happen in a given set of
  circumstances. If the prediction comes true, it supports the theory. If it
  doesn't, the theory may need to be revised.
5
Natural Phenomena
Science is concerned with the natural world. It does not include
  supernatural explanations in its theories or predictions.
6
Progressiveness
Science is progressive. It builds on previous knowledge to advance and
  refine understanding. New theories incorporate and build upon the predictions
  of older theories where they have been successful.
7
Tentativeness
Scientific theories are tentative. They are subject to revision and
  correction as new evidence is discovered. This is part of the self-correcting
  nature of science.
8
Parsimony (Occam's Razor)
Science prefers the simplest explanation that is consistent with the
  evidence. This principle is often referred to as Occam's Razor.
9
Systematic Exploration
All scientific inquiry is systematic, meaning it follows a specific
  plan or procedure. This often involves forming a hypothesis, testing it
  through various means such as experiments or additional observations, and
  refining or discarding the hypothesis based on the results.
10
Objectivity
Science aims to minimize bias through rigorous methodology and peer
  review. Findings are reported objectively, without personal feelings or
  beliefs influencing the results.
