Integration of Creative Contributions
Enduring Contributions
Darwin’s theory of natural selection exemplifies Law #1 (Integration Upwards) by embedding itself into scientific and cultural paradigms. Its integration has influenced not only biology but also social sciences, ethics, and technology. Similarly, Einstein’s theory of relativity illustrates how groundbreaking ideas integrate into physics and technology, shaping fields like GPS navigation.
Progenitors vs. Epigones
Progenitors, such as Michelangelo or Shakespeare, succeed in integrating their works into broader cultural narratives, unlike epigones, who merely imitate without achieving systemic impact. For example, Michelangelo’s sculptures like David remain central to Western art, while lesser-known imitators fail to integrate similarly.

Original Contributions
