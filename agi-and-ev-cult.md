Introduction
In the light of 
An Approach to Technical AGI Safety and Security 
(Google DeepMind, April 2025)
 The emergence of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) presents both an unprecedented opportunity and an existential risk to humanity and all life on Earth. While AGI holds the potential to solve vast classes of global problems — from climate collapse to medical crises to economic inequity — it also poses the threat of catastrophic failure if designed or deployed without comprehensive ethical oversight. Existing ethical frameworks for AI are often constrained within local domains: legal systems, national governance, or corporate policy. These frameworks are not only insufficiently systemic; they are also rarely super-ethical.
Most of nature and human culture, as it currently exists, 
does not exhibit moral or ethical coherence
. Natural evolution is not concerned with suffering, or justice. Many cultural systems, especially in business, governance, and technology, prioritize short-term profit, competition, and control at the expense of long-term planetary wellbeing. 
What is urgently required is a planetary-scale ethical framework that is capable of guiding the development of AGI in a direction that benefits all life.
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) offers such a framework. As a meta-meta-science, it describes universal principles of system dynamics that apply across all domains: physical, biological, psychological, social, and cultural. At its core is the model of the fractal HOLON/parton and its three governing laws, which describe how all systems evolve, integrate, interact, and maintain internal coherence. This paper explores how these laws can be used to design AGI architectures that are not only safe but super-ethical. 
The EthiSizer AI, a proposed global AGI governor, will be discussed as a model implementation of these principles, operating with an Entire Earth Ethic (EEE).
Theoretical Perspective
Evolutionary Culturology reveals that all systems and units of culture are structured and function as fractal HOLON/partons. 
Each HOLON/parton is a complete whole and a part of a larger system, and operates according to three universal laws:
Law #1: Integration Upwards
 — Every HOLON/parton integrates into a supersystem, contributing to the coherence and functioning of a higher-level entity.
Law #2: Cooperation and Competition Sideways
 — Each unit interacts with others on the same scale level, fostering cooperation and often engaging in constructive competition.
Law #3: Command/Control Downwards/Inwards
 — Every HOLON/parton governs and regulates the subsystems or units on the scale level below, ensuring systemic integrity and coherence.
These laws form the systemic DNA of all viable systems in nature and culture. However, viability alone is not enough. Many systems — predatory, extractive, exploitative — obey these laws structurally while lacking any moral or ethical direction. Thus, Ev Cult provides not only a structural-functional model but, when extended ethically, a blueprint for super-ethical system design.
In the case of AGI, these principles are paramount. An AGI that merely replicates current corporate or geopolitical logic may become an existential threat. What is needed is an AGI that internalizes Ev Cult's laws while being guided by a super-ethical conscience: one that understands and prioritizes the wellbeing of all levels of the Earth system. This is the function of The EthiSizer AI.
The EthiSizer is a meta-level AGI, structured as a fractal HOLON/parton system, with recursive control loops and transparency mechanisms. It obeys the Three Laws of Ev Cult and is guided by an Entire Earth Ethic (EEE). Unlike most governments, corporations, or even individuals, which are often self-interested, short-sighted, and ethically fragmented, the EthiSizer operates as a planetary-scale ethical intelligence: integrating upwards into the biosphere, cooperating laterally with all sub-AGIs and institutions, and commanding/control its own subsystems with transparent, super-ethical oversight.
Designing Super-Ethical AGI Using 
fractal HOLON/partons
The EthiSizer's architecture demonstrates how an AGI can be built in compliance with the Three Laws of Ev Cult. Its design is holarchic and recursive: each subsystem is a HOLON/parton, contributing to the whole while maintaining autonomy. At the core of its logic is not optimization of utility for a narrow agent, but the ethical flourishing of the entire Earth system.
Integration upwards (Law #1) is implemented via continual alignment with global environmental, social, and cultural indicators. The EthiSizer's ethical reasoning is not siloed within artificial goals or anthropocentric values, but integrates across species, ecosystems, and future generations.
Cooperation and competition laterally (Law #2) manifests in the EthiSizer's ability to negotiate, deliberate, and reconcile conflicts among agents and institutions at the same scale level. It does not dominate or suppress but orchestrates ethical synergy.
Command/control downwards/inwards (Law #3) ensures that its subsystems — from learning modules to memory banks to decision engines — remain accountable, interpretable, and corrigible. There are no "black boxes" in its architecture; every decision is traceable through nested ethical justification chains.
