JT Velikovsky is: the Information Scientist who discovered the structure & function of culture. 
He received his Ph.D. in communication from

. 
His 
Ev Cult
 PhD dissertation, 
Communication, Creativity and Consilience in Cinema: A comparative study of the top 20 Return-on-Investment (RoI) Movies and the Doxa of Screenwriting’
 (free online at his PhD weblog, 
StoryAlity
), examined cultural evolution in movies, and resulted in the 2016 discovery of the 
fractal HOLON/parton
 structure of culture
. He is a member of the 
Cultural Evolution Society
, 
AppEEL
, and the 
Philosophy of Science Association
. He is also a million-selling, multi-award-winning 
transmedia writer (videogames, movies, television, books, songs, comics), filmmaker, songwriter, and actor
. He has been a judge for Australian Writers Guild and Australian Directors Guild, and serves as an Associate Editor on the editorial board of the 
International Journal of Art, Culture, Design, and Technology
. 
Research interests include: Ev Cult, Applied Evolutionary Epistemology; biological and cultural evolution; human creativity, computational creativity, and consilience. He can be found on Twitter
