Abstract
Current educational systems fragment knowledge across siloed subjects, impeding students’ ability to understand the interrelations among domains. Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), a meta-meta-science of everything, offers a unifying framework based on the 
fractal HOLON/parton 
structure of all systems and cultural units. This paper outlines how Ev Cult can be practically applied to restructure K–12 and higher education curricula, enabling transdisciplinary learning, systemic coherence, and deep consilience. We demonstrate how teaching through the 3 Laws of 
fractal HOLON/partons 
creates scaffolds for understanding across all domains of knowledge—from physics to the arts—and how this approach better reflects the structure of reality. A proposed curriculum model is introduced, along with pedagogical strategies and implementation pathways.
Keywords
Evolutionary Culturology; Education Reform; fractal HOLON/partons; Systems Thinking; Transdisciplinary Learning; Consilience; Curriculum Design
Introduction
The prevailing structure of formal education divides knowledge into discrete subjects, often with little effort to demonstrate their interconnectedness. While specialization has allowed for the deep development of disciplines, it has come at the cost of integration and the learner's ability to see patterns across domains. This compartmentalization hinders the cultivation of systems thinking, ethical reasoning, and the capacity to solve complex, "wicked" problems that span multiple domains.
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) proposes a solution to this issue. As a meta-meta-science, Ev Cult identifies the universal unit of analysis across all domains: the fractal HOLON/parton. This unit, governed by three foundational laws, is observable in all systems, whether physical, biological, psychological, or sociocultural. By reorienting educational content around these structures and principles, we can achieve a curriculum that not only delivers domain-specific knowledge but fosters holistic, consilient understanding.
Theoretical Perspective: The 3 Laws of 
fractal HOLON/partons 
in Ev Cult
The fractal HOLON/parton is a concept that synthesizes Koestler's holons, Feynman's partons, and Mandelbrot's fractals. Each HOLON/parton is a complete whole and a part simultaneously. 
Ev Cult identifies three universal laws that govern these structures:
Law #1 – Integration Upwards:
 Every HOLON/parton is nested within a larger supersystem. For example, a cell integrates into a tissue, which integrates into an organ. This principle ensures systemic coherence and upward emergence.
Law #2 – Cooperation and Competition Sideways:
 Units on the same scale-level interact laterally. They cooperate to maintain the system's integrity and often compete for resources or dominance. This dynamic interplay drives adaptation, differentiation, and innovation.
Law #3 – Command and Control Downwards:
 Each HOLON/parton exercises control over its internal subsystems. This principle underlies regulation, feedback loops, and organizational integrity within any system.
 
When these three laws are applied as analytical tools across educational domains, they reveal deep isomorphies. 
A molecule, a musical note, a word, or a social norm can all be understood as fractal HOLON/partons within their respective systems, and thus taught using a unified epistemology.
The Ev Cult Worldview:
Diagnosing the Problem: Fragmentation in Current Curricula
Most educational systems segment knowledge into disconnected subjects with arbitrary boundaries. Students learn algebra in isolation from physics, biology without reference to systems theory, literature without grounding in Evolutionary Psychology, Evolutionary Sociology, or even Evolutionary Culturology (all domains). This fragmentation leads to several issues:
Lack of transferability of knowledge across contexts
Weak systems thinking and problem-solving skills
Redundancy and inefficiency in curriculum design
Failure to equip students to understand complex global challenges
Even interdisciplinary and STEAM initiatives, while well-meaning, often lack a foundational framework that explains why and how different domains connect. Without a unifying theory, such efforts remain ad hoc and superficial.
Ev Cult’s Solution: Structuring Education According to Fractal HOLON/partons
Ev Cult offers a principled approach to curriculum integration. By teaching all content through the lens of the 3 Laws, educators can foster a systems-level understanding in students from the earliest years. Instead of treating subjects as silos, we treat them as domains composed of nested, interacting units that follow the same structural laws.
Students learn to recognize that:
Atoms integrate into molecules (Law #1), just as notes integrate into chords.
Organs cooperate within a body (Law #2), just as political institutions or ecosystems do.
Words control letters (Law #3), just as ideologies govern beliefs.
This approach reveals the deep unity underlying all knowledge and prepares students to transfer insights across domains. For example, understanding how feedback loops function in biological systems can help students grasp similar dynamics in economic systems or narrative structures.

Conclusion
Evolutionary Culturology offers a transformative vision for education reform. By re-structuring curricula around the 3 Laws of fractal HOLON/partons, we can unify fragmented disciplines and build a generation of learners capable of navigating and solving complex global challenges. This reform does not discard existing subject matter, but recontextualizes it within a broader framework that mirrors the structure of reality itself. In doing so, Ev Cult not only delivers content but cultivates consilience, ethical systems thinking, and a lifelong capacity for integrated learning.
