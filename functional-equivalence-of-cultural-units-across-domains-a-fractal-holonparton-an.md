Introduction
 
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) is a meta-meta-scientific framework that solves the problem of consilience by identifying universal units of structure and function—fractal HOLON/partons—across all domains of knowledge. 
These units obey three Laws: upward integration into larger systems (Law #1), lateral cooperation and competition (Law #2), and downward command and control over sub-units (Law #3). 
Rather than identifying units based on material substance or absolute scale, Ev Cult classifies them functionally, relative to their position within their system’s hierarchy. 
This enables rigorous comparison of units in seemingly unrelated domains—from atoms to alphabets, brushstrokes to binary bits. Prior attempts at cross-domain mapping (e.g., in semiotics, systems theory, or comparative linguistics) have lacked a unifying framework grounded in universal system principles. Ev Cult provides this framework.
