0. Introduction
The study of intelligence has long been fragmented across disciplines. Neuroscience investigates biological intelligence, cognitive science studies human cognition, and artificial intelligence (AI) research focuses on synthetic intelligence. Despite their differences, these domains all explore problem-solving, adaptation, and information processing.
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) unifies these perspectives under a single scientific framework. By treating intelligence as an evolutionary process governed by the three laws of fractal HOLON/partons, Ev Cult offers a model where intelligence emerges, evolves, and integrates across hierarchical levels. 
This paper introduces an
