Discussion/Analysis of the Table
 
Above
The table above presents
a detailed hierarchy of 
30 scale levels of communication as per Evolutionary
Culturology
, illustrating how 
information exchange
 (communication) occurs at different levels
of complexity, from the fundamental substrates of the universe, up to the multiverse. 
This hierarchy follows the 
first Three Laws of fractal HOLON/partons
, revealing: (1) integration upwards, (2) lateral cooperation and competition, and (3) command and
control downwards/inwards.
Law 1 - Integration Upwards
Each scale level integrates into a larger unit above it,
forming a continuous hierarchy of complexity. For example, molecules integrate
into macromolecules, which further integrate into organelles, and so forth.
This integration facilitates the 
emergence
 of higher-order properties, and
systems.
 
Law 2 - Cooperation and Competition Laterally/Sideways
At each scale level, units cooperate and compete with their
peers. For instance, cells within tissues 
cooperate
 to maintain tissue function - but may also 
compete 
for resources! This lateral interaction drives evolution
and adaptation, within each scale level.
 
Law 3 - Command and Control Downwards
Each unit commands and controls the smaller units within it,
ensuring the regulation and coordination necessary for maintaining structure
and function. For example, organ systems control organs, which in turn control
tissues.

first Three Laws of fractal HOLON/partons
 also result in
