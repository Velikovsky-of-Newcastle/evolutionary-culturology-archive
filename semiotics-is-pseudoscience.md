Introduction
Semiotics, the study of signs and symbols and their use in communication, has been a dominant framework in so-called "Cultural Theory", throughout the 20th century. 
Developed by old, dead, and outdated folks like Ferdinand de Saussure, and Charles Sanders Peirce, and later elaborated by wacky theorists such as Roland Barthes and Umberto Eco, semiotics has (sadly) influenced linguistics, literary theory, media studies, and, the arts. 
However, despite its popularity, semiotics never matured into a scientific discipline. 
And why?
Because it lacks: falsifiability, testability, empirical rigor, and scalability. It's all wrong.
Instead, it has remained a hermeneutic and philosophical method, confined to human symbolic culture.
This paper reveals that semiotics is not a science, but, a pseudoscientific relic of pre-systems thinking. 
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), by contrast, is a meta-meta-scientific paradigm that unifies knowledge across all domains through the concept of 
fractal HOLON/partons 
and three universal laws of system behavior. 
Ev Cult not only provides a scientific foundation for understanding cultural meaning and structure, but also renders semiotics obsolete.
Theoretical Perspective 
Ev Cult is based on the principle that all units in all domains—physical, biological, cultural—are structured as fractal HOLON/partons. 
These are simultaneously wholes and parts within nested systems. The behavior of fractal HOLON/partons follows three universal laws:
Law #1: Fractal HOLON/partons integrate upwards into supersystems. 
Law #2: Fractal HOLON/partons cooperate and compete laterally with others at the same scale level. 
Law #3: Fractal HOLON/partons command/control downwards/inwards, the smaller systems on the scale level below, which are inside them.

These laws provide a causal, structural, and evolutionary model of all systems, including culture. Unlike semiotics, which is anthropocentric and arbitrary, Ev Cult provides a scientifically valid and universally applicable framework for modeling meaning and information evolution. For example, in biology, genes are fractal HOLON/partons within genomes, which in turn reside within cells. In language, phonemes form morphemes, then words, then sentences. These are testable, causal hierarchies—not arbitrary relations.
Why Semiotics Is a Pseudoscience 
Semiotics fails to meet the criteria of science. It is not falsifiable or testable. It does not offer predictive or causal models. It lacks grounding in systems theory, evolutionary dynamics, or information science. Instead, it treats meaning as an arbitrary relationship between signifiers and signifieds, or between symbols and referents. Yikes.
Semiotics is confined to human cultural texts, symbols, and codes. It does not scale across domains. It cannot account for the structure of meaning in biology, physics, or chemistry. For contrast, the DNA molecule in biology is a physical structure that encodes functional information through testable mechanisms of transcription, translation, and inheritance. Semiotics has no such operational equivalents. It relies on interpretation, not explanation. It substitutes analogy for mechanism.
Ev Cult exposes these limitations and replaces them with a rigorous framework grounded in system dynamics, evolution, and universal structure.

Cultural Meaning Re-Defined by Evolutionary Culturology 
Ev Cult reconceptualizes meaning not as arbitrary signification - but, as a systemic function of evolutionary integration, cooperation/competition, and downward control. 
Cultural units are fractal HOLON/partons, embedded in larger systems. Their meaning emerges from their structural position, evolutionary history, and system dynamics.
Meaning is no longer static or assigned; it is emergent, dynamic, and testable. It is not dependent on subjective interpretation, but on objective systemic roles. Cultural phenomena such as words, rituals, and symbols are not mere signs; they are outputs of nested, evolving systems that can be modeled, measured, and predicted. 
For example, a national anthem is not just a "symbol" but a cultural fractal HOLON/parton within a system of national identity, composed of inputs (musical tradition, language), processors (institutional adoption, performance), and outputs (civic unity, emotional resonance).
The Persistence of Semiotics: Why It Lasted So Long 
Semiotics persisted, due to the absence of a formal, scientific alternative. 
In the absence of systems theory and cultural evolution models, scholars gravitated to semiotics for its surface-level explanatory appeal. Its flexibility allowed broad application across the humanities.
However, this very flexibility is its weakness. It lacks constraint, formal rigor, or empirical discipline. The academic humanities often preferred interpretive over scientific methods, which allowed semiotics to flourish without challenge. Evolutionary Culturology now corrects this imbalance. Since its formalization in the 2016 doctoral thesis on Evolutionary Culturology, and subsequent works such as Elements of Evolutionary Culturology (2023), this framework has provided the first empirically grounded, domain-general alternative.

Eco's "Theory of Semiotics" presents taxonomies of signs but offers no causal mechanisms or system dynamics. His model remains abstract and detached from embodied or evolutionary processes. Ev Cult replaces sign taxonomies with domain-general modeling of processors and outputs in nested systems.

Deleuze and Guattari reject hierarchical structure in favor of "rhizomes."
