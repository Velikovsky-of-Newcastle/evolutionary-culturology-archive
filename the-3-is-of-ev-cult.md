Core Meta-meta-scientific Principles, and, the 
fractal HOLON/parton
At the core of Ev Cult is the scientific revelation that: All entities in the universe are both wholes and parts simultaneously—a property captured by the term “fractal HOLON/parton.”

(Law #1) Incorporation ↑, 
(Law #2) Interaction ←→ , and 
(Law #3) Influence ↓
1.
 
Incorporation
 (Integration Upwards, in scale-level ↑): Smaller units integrate upwards in scale, into larger whole units. This law explains how cells form tissues, words form sentences, and individual ideas coalesce into complex cultural systems.
2.
 
Interaction 
(Lateral Collaboration (or, Cooperation] and Conflict [Competition]) Laterally (←→): Units at the same scale interact, by both 
co-operating 
(or “joining forces”; or “collaborating”), and 
competing

3.
 
Influence Inwards 
(or Command and Control, Downwards in scale) (↓): Larger systems govern (or direct) the behavior of the smaller units within them, as seen in how how societal norms shape individual behavior, or how a book structures the arrangement of its chapters and sentences.
 
Together, these 3 universal laws provide a universal scale-independent meta-meta-scientific framework, that accounts for the structural and dynamic (functional/behavioral) similarities across all disciplines.
