The 9 P's of Creativity, in Evolutionary Culturology
In exploring the interplay of Science and Art through the lens of Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), a critical tool emerges: 
The 9 P's of Creativity
. 
This framework allows for a comprehensive understanding of how creative endeavors, encompassing both scientific innovations and artistic masterpieces, evolve from complex geo-bio-psycho-socio-cultural systems. 
By applying this model to the polymaths listed in our table, we gain deeper insights into the creative processes that have shaped our cultural landscape.
1. Creative Problem: Each individual in our table confronted unique problems. For example, Leonardo da Vinci explored the problem of human anatomy with an artist's eye, leading to scientific discoveries. The nature of these problems varied from presented (posed by external circumstances), discovered (identified through exploration), or created (formulated by the individual themselves).
2. Creative Product: The products of these individuals’ creativity range from scientific theories to artworks. Each product serves as a unit of culture, a 
fractal HOLON/parton

3. Creative Person (or Agent/AI): The personalities behind these innovations and creations were diverse, from solitary thinkers like Nikola Tesla to collaborative geniuses like the scientist-artist Leonardo da Vinci.
