A

In engineering, everything from a bolt to a turbine, or a line of code to an operating system, is a fractal HOLON/parton (Velikovsky, 2016; Koestler, 1967).
Ev Cult’s Three Laws
All fractal HOLON/partons—and therefore, all 
engineered systems

Law #1 – Integration Upwards
: Every unit functions as a subsystem within a larger supersystem, contributing to its emergence and integrity.
Law #2 – Cooperation and Competition Sideways
: Every unit interacts laterally with other units on the same scale level, forming functional interfaces and conflict boundaries.
Law #3 – Command and Control Downwards
: Every unit governs its internal subcomponents, imposing structure and processing control flows.
These laws are not prescriptive - but 
descriptive

While Systems Engineering emphasizes structure and feedback, Ev Cult additionally foregrounds the
