The PhD dissertation investigates fifteen (15) key guidelines of the screenwriting orthodoxy, comparing them against the traits of the top 20 highest, and also 20 lowest RoI ( Audience Reach / Production Budget) movies​​.
Structured into various chapters, it includes: operational definitions of creativity, the systems model of creativity, and the screenwriting doxa; a theoretical perspective utilizing an evolutionary systems view (the Ev Cult 
fractal HOLON/parton

The research answers five key questions, including the nature of the current orthodoxy in movie screenwriting, and the characteristics of the most and least successful movies, in terms of RoI (movie audience reach, divided by film producton budget)​​.
The study's key contributions include a novel method of direct content data analysis, critical analysis of the screenwriting doxa (popular screenwriting manuals), identification of key patterns in the emergence of top and bottom RoI movies, and the development of a new set of heuristics for movie story creation, termed `The StoryAlity Theory​​'. 
It also draws upon the work of scholars such as David Bordwell and Brian Boyd, applying concepts like Popperian 
creative problem/solution models 
in filmmaking and 
artistic cost/benefit ratios

This research provides valuable insights into the screenwriting process, challenging many existing norms, and contributing radical new methodologies and scientific theories to the domain of movie creation and screenwriting.

The Ev Cult PhD Explainer
The "Ev Cult PhD" dissertation by J T Velikovsky, (also known as Velikovsky of Newcastle), titled "Communication, Creativity and Consilience in Cinema: A comparative study of the top 20 Return-on-Investment (RoI) Movies and the Doxa of Screenwriting," addresses the challenge of determining accurate guidelines for movie success. 
The study focuses on the domain of movie creation, including screenwriting, and explores the effectiveness of 15 key screenwriting guidelines from contemporary manuals, which make up the current orthodoxy or 'doxa' of screenwriting​​.
The study's central Research Question revolves around the existence of formulae for movie success, considering the screenplay as a critical factor. It examines the use of data-mining, neural networks, and deep-learning algorithms by professional movie story consultants like Epagogix, which analyze screenplay elements to predict a movie's Return-on-Investment​​.
The study Methodology involves a direct content analysis of the two extremes in movie RoI – the top 20 and bottom 20 – to empirically determine the truth-value of the recommendations found in popular screenwriting manuals. The goal is to use movie return-on-investment (RoI), defined as 'audience-reach / production-budget,' as a criterion for success​​.
Significant contributions of this research include: introducing a novel method for direct content data analysis, to compare Top and Bottom 20 RoI movie narratives, critically analyzing the current screenwriting guidelines, identifying new patterns in the emergence of top and bottom 20 RoI movies, and developing a new set of 27 heuristics for movie story and screenplay creation, named the StoryAlity Theory​​.
