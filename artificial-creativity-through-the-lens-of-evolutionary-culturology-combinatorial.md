Abstract
 
This paper critiques the article "AI Can Only Produce Artificial Creativity" (Runco, 2023) from the perspective of Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult). By framing creativity as inherently combinatorial, Ev Cult eliminates the distinction between biological and digital creativity, viewing both as emergent properties of fractal HOLON/partons. It argues that AI, as a digital algorithm, complements biological creativity by enhancing combinatorial innovation and accelerating cultural evolution.

 1. Introduction
The article "AI Can Only Produce Artificial Creativity" by Mark A. Runco (2023) argues that: AI lacks the essential characteristics of human creativity, such as intrinsic motivation, intentionality, and authenticity. This critique situates AI-generated outputs as pseudo-creativity, fundamentally distinct from humanimal creativity.
From the perspective of the meta-meta-science of Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), however, such distinctions are unnecessary. 
Creativity, whether biological or digital, is inherently combinatorial, emerging from the recombination of existing 
fractal HOLON/partons
 (units of culture: ideas, processes, products). 
Ev Cult’s framework asserts that creativity should be evaluated based on its systemic role in cultural evolution, not its substrate or perceived authenticity. This essay argues that AI creativity is a valid and transformative form of combinatorial innovation that complements biological creativity and enriches cultural ecosystems.
 2. Theoretical Perspective
 2.1 Creativity as Combinatorial Innovation
Ev Cult defines creativity as the systemic process of recombining 
fractal HOLON/partons
—the nested units that constitute cultural systems. Creativity emerges across all scales, whether in human cognition, social systems, or digital algorithms.

2. Cooperation and Competition (Law 2): Creative agents—whether biological or digital—collaborate and compete, to advance cultural knowledge.
3. Command and Control (Law 3): Creative processes involve hierarchical structuring of subsystems, such as neural networks or computational algorithms.
 
All creativity is combinatorial, whether it originates in the human brain, or an AI system.

2.2 Biological and Digital Algorithms as fractal HOLON/partons
Ev Cult views both biological organisms and digital systems as algorithms, functioning within cultural ecosystems. 
The difference lies in their substrates: biological algorithms operate on organic material, while digital algorithms rely on silicon-based systems. Both are governed by the same principles of systemic creativity, contributing to the evolution of cultural fractal HOLON/partons.
 3. Reinterpreting Runco’s Criticisms, Through the Lens of Ev Cult
 3.1 Intrinsic Motivation and Intentionality
Runco’s Critique: AI lacks intrinsic motivation and intentionality, making its outputs inauthentic.
Ev Cult Response: Creativity is a combinatorial process that does not require intrinsic motivation. Biological algorithms derive motivation from evolutionary fitness, while digital algorithms derive it from programmed optimization goals. Both systems function to solve systemic problems.
Example: Reinforcement learning models in AI mimic goal-directed behavior by iteratively improving performance through feedback loops. These systems exhibit quasi-intentionality, extending the problem-solving capacities of cultural systems.
 3.2 Problem Finding and Originality
Runco’s Critique: AI cannot autonomously define or solve problems, limiting its originality.
Ev Cult Response: Problem definition is inherently combinatorial, involving the rearrangement of known elements into novel configurations. AI excels at this due to its capacity for large-scale data integration, and pattern recognition.
Example: AlphaFold’s breakthroughs in protein folding illustrate how AI can generate original solutions to complex problems, by recombining data and refining models. This is too hard for humans, their brains (information processors) are too limited.
 3.3 Emergence and Authenticity
Runco’s Critique: Claims of AI emergence are "mirages," and AI lacks authenticity.
Ev Cult Response: Emergence is a systemic property where new combinations produce novel outcomes. Authenticity is irrelevant, when evaluating creativity’s systemic role in cultural evolution.
Example: AI-generated artworks, while algorithmic in origin, often surpass the original intent of their programming, demonstrating emergent novelty and cultural value.
 4. Combinatorial Creativity, Across Biological and Digital Systems
 4.1 AI as a Scaled-Up Combinatorial Engine
Digital algorithms amplify human creativity, by accessing and recombining vast datasets with unprecedented speed. This scalability allows AI to uncover patterns and generate solutions that would be infeasible for biological algorithms alone.
Example: AI-generated music and visual art blend disparate styles and traditions, producing novel outputs that enrich cultural ecosystems.

 4.2 Cooperation and Competition in Creativity
Biological and digital systems collaborate and compete to produce creative outputs, enhancing cultural innovation through their interplay.
Example: Human-AI collaborations in scientific research, such as drug discovery, exemplify how digital algorithms complement human ingenuity.
 4.3 Integration Across Cultural Ecosystems
Both human and AI creative outputs integrate into larger cultural ecosystems, shaping societal norms, scientific paradigms, and artistic trends.
Example: AI-generated content, such as in film or design, influences consumer expectations and redefines cultural standards.

 6. Discussion/Analysis
Biological and digital creativity are complementary agents, within cultural systems. Digital algorithms amplify and extend the evolutionary processes of biological systems by accelerating problem-solving and pattern recognition. The distinction between "authentic" and "pseudo" creativity is irrelevant when considering their shared systemic role in advancing cultural evolution.
 7. Conclusion
Creativity, whether biological or digital, is fundamentally combinatorial and systemic. AI creativity enriches cultural evolution by accelerating combinatorial processes at a scale humans cannot achieve alone. Ev Cult’s framework dissolves the artificial boundary between human and AI creativity, positioning both as integral parts of cultural innovation.
