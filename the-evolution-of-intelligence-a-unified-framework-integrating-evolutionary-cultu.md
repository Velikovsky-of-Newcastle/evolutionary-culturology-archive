From the development of basic neural systems in simple organisms to the complex cognition of humans and the cultural tools they create, intelligence represents the cumulative adaptation of systems to process, store, and apply information effectively. 
While Natural Intelligence (NI) focuses on the biological substrates of cognition, and Cognitive Informatics (CI) explores the computational mechanisms of natural and artificial intelligence, these domains often lack a unifying framework to explain intelligence evolution across scales. 
Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult), with its foundational principles of fractal HOLON/partons, provides a meta-meta-scientific perspective that connects these domains. 
This paper integrates Ev Cult, CI, and NI to explore the evolution of intelligence, emphasizing its implications for autonomic computing, neuroinformatics, and hybrid intelligence systems.
