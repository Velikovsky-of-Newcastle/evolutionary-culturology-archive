TABLE KEY to Phenetic Traits:
The key below shows the coding system used in the table above.
Observable Form:
1: The unit can be directly observed or experienced.
0: The unit cannot be directly observed or experienced.
Usage Frequency:
1: The unit is frequently used or encountered.
0: The unit is rarely used or encountered.
Impact on Society:
1: The unit has a significant impact on society.
0: The unit has little to no impact on society.
Integration with Other Units:
1: The unit integrates well with other units or domains.
0: The unit does not integrate well with other units or domains.
Global Reach:
1: The unit has a global reach or influence.
0: The unit has limited or localized reach.
Temporal Existence:
1: The unit exists within a specific time frame.
0: The unit is timeless or exists across multiple time frames.
Cultural Context:
1: The unit is specific to a particular culture.
0: The unit is universal or transcends cultural boundaries.
Interactivity:
1: The unit can be interacted with.
0: The unit cannot be interacted with.
Complexity:
1: The unit consists of multiple sub-units.
0: The unit is a singular entity.
Physicality:
1: The unit has a physical location or manifestation.
0: The unit does not have a physical location or manifestation.
Narrative Function:
1: The unit serves a storytelling purpose.
0: The unit does not serve a storytelling purpose.
Ethical/Moral Implications:
1: The unit carries ethical or moral weight.
0: The unit does not carry ethical or moral weight.
Historical Significance:
1: The unit has historical importance.
0: The unit does not have historical importance.
Medium of Expression:
1: The unit has a specific medium through which it is expressed.
0: The unit does not have a specific medium of expression.
Temporal Flexibility:
1: The unit can exist or be experienced in multiple time frames.
0: The unit exists or is experienced in a single time frame.
Fictionality:
1: The unit is fictional or exists within a fictional context.
0: The unit is non-fictional.
