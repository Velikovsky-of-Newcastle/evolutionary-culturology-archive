Introduction
Throughout history, humans have grappled with the sheer complexity of existence. From the intricate structures of galaxies to the fine-tuned mechanisms of biological life, the universe appears as an endless hierarchy of interconnected systems. 
Scientists have responded by devising reductionist methods to simplify this complexity. Mathematicians developed Category Theory, which treats mathematical objects as mere points connected by morphisms, focusing not on the internal composition of these objects but rather on their relationships. Similarly, Evolutionary Culturology dismantles systems, revealing them as networks of fractal HOLON/partons—entities that are simultaneously whole and part of a larger whole.
This paper examines how the meta-meta-science of Ev Cult serves as a universal analytical tool, akin to Category Theory, and how it enables us to understand the deep structure of cultural and scientific systems.
