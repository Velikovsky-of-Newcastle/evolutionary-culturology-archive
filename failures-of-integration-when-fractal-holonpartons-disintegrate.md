Introduction
 
The meta-meta-science of Evolutionary Culturology (Ev Cult) proposes that all domains and systems evolve through the interaction of three statistical laws governing fractal HOLON/partons. 
The Ev Cult Worldview:
Law #1 asserts that these units tend to integrate upwards into larger supersystems. Yet, crucially, the laws are not deterministic; they express tendencies, and these tendencies sometimes fail. 
This essay focuses on 
the breakdown of Law #1—the failure of systems to integrate
—and interprets these failures not as anomalies, but as statistically consistent phases within a broader systemic cycle. Disintegration is a necessary complement to integration, and often precedes renewal. 
Through biological, sociological, and economic case studies, and in conjunction with Panarchy Theory, this paper briefly investigates how and why 
integration
 fails.
Theoretical Perspective
 
The meta-meta-science of Ev Cult identifies all units across all domains as fractal HOLON/partons—units that are simultaneously wholes and parts, structured hierarchically. 
These units behave according to three statistical laws:
Law #1:
 They integrate upwards into supersystems.
Law #2:
 They cooperate or compete laterally with units on the same level.
Law #3:
 They command and control units nested below (inside) them.
These laws apply not only to physical systems (atoms to molecules to organisms) but also to mental, cultural, economic, and ecological structures. 
Because the laws are statistical, they allow for cases of disintegration, adversarial dynamics, and failure of control. Such breakdowns are integral to a system's evolution and can be structured and predicted.
Panarchy Theory: Cycles of Integration and Disintegration
 
Panarchy Theory (Gunderson & Holling, 2002) introduces a four-phase adaptive cycle observed in complex systems:
r (Growth/Exploitation)
K (Conservation/Stability)
Ω (Collapse/Release)
α (Reorganization/Renewal)
These phases mirror the structural tendencies of the meta-meta-science of Ev Cult. 
Law #1 operates predominantly in r and α phases, Law #2 stabilizes systems in K and α, and Law #3 underpins both conservation (K) and reorganization (α). 
Collapse (Ω) represents the breakdown of Law #1, but also serves as a prelude to systemic transformation. Thus, Panarchy Theory offers a temporal cycle that aligns with the meta-meta-scientific spatial-hierarchical logic of Ev Cult.
