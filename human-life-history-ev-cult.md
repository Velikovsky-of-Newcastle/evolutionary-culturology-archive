Pre-Birth and Infancy
In the pre-birth stage, genetic material (DNA) serves as the initial 
fractal HOLON/partons

This genetic foundation commands and controls the developmental processes that follow (Law #3). Similarly, familial and cultural backgrounds provide the meso-level context, influencing the individual's potential and future interactions.
During infancy, biological development continues under these 3 universal Laws, with an added emphasis on the integration into family units and the immediate social system. This stage lays the foundation for the individual's physical and social identity.
Childhood, Adolescence, and Adulthood
Childhood and adolescence involve significant psychological and social development. Here, Ev Cult also aligns with 
Life History Theory
, emphasizing the trade-offs between growth, learning, and socialization. Psychological development (micro level) and socialization processes (meso level) demonstrate the integration, competition, and cooperation with peers and societal structures.
Adulthood, according to Ev Cult, is where individuals fully express their 
fractal HOLON/partons (units of culture) 
through professional skills and personal interests, family, and societal roles. This stage reflects the culmination of earlier developmental processes and the individual's capacity to influence and contribute to society.
Old Age, Legacy, and Posthumous Influence
The final stages of life - old age, and posthumous influence - represent the legacy of the individual. At this point, the accumulated experiences and wisdom become crucial in guiding younger generations (micro and meso levels). The macro level emphasizes the individual's long-term cultural impact, aligning with Life History Theory's focus on the evolutionary significance of senescence and post-reproductive life stages.
