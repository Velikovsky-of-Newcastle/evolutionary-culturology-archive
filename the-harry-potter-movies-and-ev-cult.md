Analysis of Antagonists' Plans:
The antagonists in the Harry Potter movies, such as Lord Voldemort, Tom Riddle, and others, consistently employ strategies that align with the Three Laws of 
fractal HOLON/partons:
Law #1: Integration upwards - The antagonists seek to disrupt the existing structure of the wizarding world, whether by destabilizing institutions like Hogwarts or threatening the international wizarding alliance. For example, Lord Voldemort's plan in "Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire" disrupts the global wizarding community by manipulating the Triwizard Tournament.
Law #2: Cooperation and competition - Antagonists cooperate laterally with other units on the same scale-level while also competing with them. In the films, this is evident in their use of fear, propaganda, and manipulation to incite discord within Hogwarts and the wizarding community.
Law #3: Command and control downwards - Antagonists exert control over smaller units on the scale level below them. This is reflected in their manipulation of magical artifacts, entities, and individuals to further their goals. For instance, the use of the Basilisk in "Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets" exemplifies this law.
Analysis of Harry's Actions:
Harry Potter, as the protagonist, consistently embodies the principles of Ev Cult in his actions to counter the antagonists' plans.
Law #1: Maintain stability - Harry's actions throughout the series are aimed at preserving the stability of the wizarding world. He forms alliances, seeks to protect institutions like Hogwarts, and ultimately prevents destabilizing events.
Law #2: Foster cooperation - Harry works to foster cooperation among his friends, allies, and teachers, aiming to maintain trust and unity in the face of adversity. His actions are driven by the need to overcome challenges and rivalries within the wizarding community.
Law #3: Protect and guide - Harry takes on the role of protecting those smaller units on the scale level below him, including his friends and fellow students. He actively seeks to prevent harm and guide others toward the right path.
