The reason this line pops up a lot, of course, is: In any work of art (e.g. a narrative, a novel, a film, a game, a song, etc.) you have a 
problem-situation
 (a goal.) 
Lots of them. And, some of them, recur 
a lot
. 
Story universals. 
In the same way that: 
life 
has a lot of: recurring situations. (e.g., Eating, sleeping, saying hello, having sex, etc.)
So the dialog-line solution (in 
any & every
 case) 
converges 
on: an optimal problem-solution. (If the screenwriter is doing their job right.)
i.e., Convey (communicate) the 
most amount of information i
n the shortest (and, least-ambiguous) dialog line, possible.
In this case, the 
problem to be solved
 (the 
goal 
to be 
achieved
) is, you want to 
communicate 
to the film audience, that: 
a bunch of someones
 (e.g. a horde of enemies, usually?) is: approaching. So, 
look out!
